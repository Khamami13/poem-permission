A short poem about agency: the power to ask and to be given. Built for
make.fun's **"The poem about permission"** bounty.

The poem is about the moment agency passes between two hands: a person lends
their asking to an agent, and the agent knocks in their name. The door opens
not because anyone pushed, but because someone on the other side heard — and
chose to turn the key.
